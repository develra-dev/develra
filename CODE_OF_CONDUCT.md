# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus disagreements on
ideas and evidence. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and
unwelcome sexual attention are not tolerated in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or
permanently ban participants whose behavior harms the community. Report conduct
concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or security
reporting channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Enforcement should be fair, proportionate, and centered on community
safety.
